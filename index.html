About Me

My journey in programming began during high school in 2022 through Android reverse engineering using Java, where I worked on analyzing applications and dealing with Smali files — the low-level language used internally by Android after compilation. My passion for Java pushed me to dive deeper into Android development and understand how applications and internal systems work from a practical perspective. Over time, my focus shifted from modifying applications to building scalable and maintainable ones. In 2024, while studying Business Information Systems, I fully transitioned into Flutter development, with a strong focus on Clean Architecture, performance optimization, and building polished production-ready applications.
